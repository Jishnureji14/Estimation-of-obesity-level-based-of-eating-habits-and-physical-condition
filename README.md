# Estimation of Obesity Levels Based on Eating Habits and Physical Condition

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes the relationship between lifestyle habits and obesity levels using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

The goal is to understand how factors such as physical activity, diet, screen time, water intake, and transportation habits influence obesity.

---

##  Objectives
- Study weight and BMI distribution
- Analyze physical activity impact on obesity
- Examine relationship between screen time and BMI
- Explore dietary habits and obesity levels
- Identify correlations between lifestyle factors

---

##  Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google colab

---

##  Key Findings
- Low physical activity is associated with higher obesity levels.
- Increased screen time shows a positive relationship with BMI.
- Dietary habits significantly influence weight.
- Obesity is affected by multiple lifestyle factors combined.

---

##  Conclusion
Obesity is influenced by a combination of lifestyle behaviors and demographic factors. Maintaining regular physical activity, reducing sedentary habits, and adopting healthy eating patterns are essential for obesity prevention.
